I am a Jr. Research Scientist at the Center for Data Science at New York University, where I am advised by Yann LeCun and Kyunghyun Cho.

My research interests lie at the intersection of self-supervised learning, predictive control, humanoid control and enhancing mathematical reasoning capabilities within large language models.

I completed my undergraduate studies at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi, where I conducted research under the mentorship of Aditya Nigam.
